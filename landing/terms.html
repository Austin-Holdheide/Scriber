Prices and payment

Pro costs $9 per month or $90 per year, in US dollars, charged in advance. Payments are processed by Stripe; Scribly never receives or stores your card details. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it.

Where sales tax or VAT applies to your purchase, it is added at checkout and shown before you pay. Price changes take effect at your next renewal, and you will be told before a change applies to you.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the billing portal in the app, or by replying to any receipt. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then the account returns to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted when a plan ends: your videos and transcripts stay where they are until you delete them yourself.

Refunds

Cancelling stops future payments but does not refund the period already paid for. If something has genuinely gone wrong — you were charged twice, the service was unusable, or you subscribed by mistake and have not used the account — email hello@scribly.cc within 14 days of the charge and it will be refunded. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Failed payments

If a payment fails, Stripe retries it and we email you. Pro continues for 7 days after the failed payment so that a card that expired overnight does not lock you out mid-project. If payment has not succeeded by then, the account returns to the Free plan and is upgraded again as soon as payment succeeds.

Fair use

Per-file limits (size and length) always apply and are shown in the app.

If a single account's usage is far outside what a person could reasonably consume, we may ask you to slow down or move to a different arrangement.
