Privacy

Draft — last updated 29 October 2026

This is a short, plain-language draft for the early-access period. It will be replaced by a reviewed policy before public launch.

What we store

  • Your account email address and a password hash (we never store your password itself).
  • The video or audio files you upload, plus the transcripts, speaker labels and export files generated from them.
  • Basic operational records: when a job ran, how long it took, and any error it produced.

Where it is stored

Scribly runs on privately operated hardware. Your files stay on that server and its backups; they are not sent to a third-party transcription or AI service.

How long we keep it

Files stay until you delete them. Deleting a video removes the media, its transcript, exports and share links. Backups roll off automatically within a few weeks. Free-tier accounts may be subject to an automatic retention window once paid plans launch; that limit will be stated clearly before it applies.

Sharing

Share links are unlisted: anyone with a link can view that transcript and play the media. Revoking or deleting the link stops access immediately. We do not publish, index or resell your content.

Analytics

No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are embedded in the app or on this site.

Your choices

You can delete any video or your account at any time. To request an export or full deletion, email privacy@scribly.cc.
