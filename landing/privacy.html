Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Scribly is a small independent service, not an advertising business. Your recordings are not used to train anything, are not sent to a third-party transcription or AI provider, and are never sold or shared for marketing.

Who is responsible

Scribly is operated by an individual developer in the United States, who is the data controller for the information described here. Questions, requests and complaints: privacy@scribly.cc.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, a password hash (never the password itself), and whether the address has been confirmed.
  • Your files: the video or audio you upload, and the transcripts, speaker labels, thumbnails and export files generated from them.
  • Operational records: when a job ran, how long it took, its status, errors, and the allowance it used from your plan.
  • Billing: your plan, subscription status, renewal date, and identifiers for your Stripe customer and subscription. Card numbers never reach Scribly.

Payments

Pro is paid for through Stripe. You enter card details on Stripe's own page, so we never see or store a card number, CVC or full billing address. Stripe sends us the customer and subscription identifiers, the plan, the status and the renewal date, and it emails your receipts. Stripe processes payment data as our processor; their handling is described in Stripe's own privacy policy. If you want to know exactly which payment details exist, ask us and we will tell you what our records show.

Everything else — the database, the media files, the transcription workers and the push notification server — runs on hardware we operate ourselves. Your recordings are not uploaded to any third-party transcription or AI service.

Where it is stored

On privately operated servers and their backups. Cloudflare and Stripe are based in the United States, so some metadata and payment records are processed there. If you are in the EU or UK, that transfer relies on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Your files stay until you delete them — nothing is deleted automatically today, on any plan. Deleting a video removes the media, its transcript, exports and share links. Backups roll off automatically within a few weeks. If an automatic retention window is ever introduced for the Free plan, it will be stated here and in the app before it applies, and it will not apply retrospectively to files already stored.

Billing records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require, even after you delete your account.

Cookies and local storage

No advertising or analytics trackers are embedded in the app or on this site. The app stores your login session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in, and nothing else.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to the servers is restricted to the operator, media files are served through signed, expiring links rather than open paths, and share links can be revoked or set to expire. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, please report it to hello@scribly.cc.

Your choices

You can delete any video, revoke any share link, or delete your account at any time. You can also ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or object to a particular use. Write to privacy@scribly.cc and you will get an answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, the date at the top changes and account holders are told by email before the change takes effect.
